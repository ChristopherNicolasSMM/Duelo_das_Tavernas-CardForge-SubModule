# Fermento & Honra — Prompts de Ilustração v3

## Sistema em 4 camadas (Master Style · Deck Identity · Card Concept · Avoid List)

Cada prompt final é a concatenação de: **(1)** o Master Style abaixo, fixo pra todas as 100 cartas — é a coluna vertebral que mantém as 6 decks parecendo o mesmo jogo; **(2)** a Identidade do Deck (paleta + tipo de energia); **(3)** o Conceito da Carta, específico — sempre mostrando uma **manifestação visual do efeito**, não só uma cena genérica; **(4)** a lista do que evitar, pra barrar estilos incompatíveis e texto/runas legíveis inventadas pela IA.

### 🎨 Master Style (fixo em todas as cartas)

```
Dark fantasy TCG card illustration, semi-realistic digital fantasy painting, richly textured painterly artwork, gothic medieval tavern atmosphere, mystical arcane fantasy, cinematic composition, dramatic low-key lighting, deep blacks and dark shadows, luminous supernatural energy emerging from the main subject, strong atmospheric depth, subtle volumetric light, glowing magical particles, detailed organic textures, aged wood, stone, metal, parchment and candlelight when appropriate, sophisticated fantasy artwork, mature collectible card game aesthetic, visually rich but readable composition, strong central focal point, dynamic flowing shapes, magical manifestation of the card's effect integrated naturally into the scene, high detail around the focal subject and softer atmospheric background, handcrafted fantasy painting feeling, cohesive premium TCG artwork.
```

### 🚫 Avoid List (fixo em todas as cartas)

```
No anime, no cartoon, no Pixar/Disney style, no comic book, no cel shading, no vector art, no flat illustration, no overly clean concept art, no photography, no photorealism, no 3D render, no posed videogame-cover characters, no crowded scenes with many characters, no overly bright background, no pure historical medieval realism, no gore or grotesque horror, no steampunk, no sci-fi elements. No text, no typography, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no UI, no card frame, no border, no watermark, no readable runes or symbols — abstract occult markings only, illegible mystical shapes.
```
